Leiden Weibo Corpus
-------------------
Source: Daan van Esch, Leiden Weibo Corpus
https://leidenweibocorpus.nl/
Download documentation: https://leidenweibocorpus.nl/openaccess
Original word archive: https://leidenweibocorpus.nl/download?file=words

The corpus contains 5,103,566 Sina Weibo messages posted in January 2012.
The original downloadable tables were updated March 20, 2012.

The bundled LWC-word-frequencies.zip is a derived table under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.
It retains all word forms and occurrence counts, combines duplicate word forms,
and omits the original word IDs and reversed spellings. Metadata records the
source archive's SHA-256 and the total token count used for normalization.

Word rankings and example selections in this export use frequency data from
the Leiden Weibo Corpus. Reuse of that data and its adaptations is subject to
attribution, noncommercial use, and share-alike terms under the linked license.
